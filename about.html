Meet the Founder

Hayden Kee

Hayden Kee brings over a decade of experience in project management, process development, and process improvement across a wide range of industries, from construction and agriculture to manufacturing, analytical laboratories, R&D, applied technology, and process technology development.

Raised in Modesto, California, Hayden has deep roots in the Central Valley's agricultural community. That upbringing instilled a practical, hands-on perspective that shapes everything about how Kee Process Solutions operates today.

"Every business — large or small — has untapped potential waiting to be unlocked. My goal is to help organizations become more resilient, efficient, and sustainable by improving process flow and developing the people who run those processes every day."

Hayden's expertise spans Lean Six Sigma, Scrum, and classic project management methodologies. Rather than forcing rigid frameworks onto your operation, the focus is always on tailoring continuous improvement solutions that fit your actual needs and the people doing the work.

Our Framework

The Clear Path Method™

Map → Identify → Align → Clear → Confirm. A focused framework for operational clarity in construction, manufacturing, agriculture, and other real-world operations.

01 - Map

Map the current process flow so everyone can see how work actually moves, where it waits, and where handoffs break down.

02 - Identify

Identify the constraints, waste, rework, and recurring decisions that quietly drain capacity from the operation.

03 - Align

Align people, priorities, metrics, and daily management routines so the team can act with shared context.

04 - Clear

Clear the bottleneck by removing friction, simplifying the workflow, and installing practical countermeasures.

05 - Confirm

Confirm measurable results, savings, ownership, and sustaining controls before the engagement closes.

Why This Approach Works

The Clear Path Method is effective because it's:

  • Focused - starts with the current flow and the real constraint
  • Practical - built for real operations, not theory
  • Measurable - ties action to cost, savings, and operational impact
  • People-centered - builds capability, not dependency
  • Sustainable - confirms results before the work is called done

This method reflects everything I've learned across construction, agriculture, labs, manufacturing, and process technology development. It is not about forcing your operation into a rigid framework. It is about creating a clear path from the problem you can feel every day to the measurable result your team can sustain.
